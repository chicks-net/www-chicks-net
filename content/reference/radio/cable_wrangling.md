Velcro or wire ties are acceptable for cable management.  

⚠️ Twist ties, rubber bands, and tape are not acceptable for keeping cables or
much else together.

## Velcro

Velcro is preferred when cables will need to be lengthened or shortened often.
If expense is not an issue Velcro is preferred for long-term cable storage to
avoid issues with drying out and maximize reuse.

## Wire ties

Wire ties are preferred where expense is a large concern.  Tails must be
clipped cleanly to avoid snags or cuts.
