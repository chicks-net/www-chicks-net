📻 Radio means a lot of different things to folks...

- [ARRL](https://www.arrl.org/) - The national association for amateur radio
- [HamStudy.org](https://hamstudy.org/) - Free practice exams and license study
- [QRZ.com](https://www.qrz.com/) - Callsign lookup and community
- [RepeaterBook](https://www.repeaterbook.com/) - Worldwide repeater directory
- [Amateur Radio Stack Exchange](https://ham.stackexchange.com/) - Q&A for hams
