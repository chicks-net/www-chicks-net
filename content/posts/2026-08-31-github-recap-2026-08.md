The July recap ended
[on a lighthouse note](/posts/2026-07-31-github-recap-2026-07/); August
pivoted to something less glamorous and arguably more important:
documentation. Also the daily data-bot PRs came back from their July
vacation, so the robots are filing paperwork again. Everything is back to
normal, where "normal" means I review a hundred-odd PRs a month and most
of my code reviewers are machines.

## Notable Pull Requests

### [chicks-home #108: document every script](https://github.com/chicks-net/chicks-home/pull/108)

2,339 additions. Two thousand, three hundred, thirty-nine lines of
documentation for every script in the home-infrastructure repo — the
most-commented PR of the month with eight review rounds. Future me is
going to find this PR and buy past me a drink. This is the least sexy
kind of engineering and somehow the one I'm most proud of this month.

### [gh-observer #419: copilot reviews join the party](https://github.com/fini-net/gh-observer/pull/419)

957 additions teaching gh-observer to display Copilot reviews like any
other running GitHub Action, with a companion PR showing them in the
watch view too. When your workflow includes a robot reviewer on every
PR, your CI watcher had better know how to draw it — now it does.

### [template-repo #300: fixing the Copilot race](https://github.com/fini-net/template-repo/pull/300)

547 additions in gh-process v8.4 to fix a genuine race condition: the
`pr_checks` logic could sample Copilot's state mid-flight and draw the
wrong conclusion. A race against a robot that reviews robots — this is
where the workflow has gotten us, and honestly the fixes are more
interesting than the features.

### [gh-observer #421: push time, not commit time](https://github.com/fini-net/gh-observer/pull/421)

554 additions making gh-observer calculate elapsed time from when you
*pushed* rather than when the commit was authored — because a commit
written Tuesday and pushed Thursday shouldn't get credit for Tuesday's
urgency. Fixes #349, and an old annoyance along with it.

Honorable mention to
[fini-infra #124](https://github.com/fini-net/fini-infra/pull/124) — a
modest 62-line modernization of the fini-logs-search instance that
nonetheless generated a seven-comment thread, some of it with an actual
human in the loop. A rare species this year.
