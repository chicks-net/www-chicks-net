[November walked back a CDN](/posts/2025-11-30-github-recap-2025-11/);
December went big instead. One hundred twenty-seven pull requests,
every last one merged — and one of them was, by a factor that
embarrasses the rest, the largest PR of my entire year: a hundred and
sixty-seven thousand lines of Google+ content, imported into this
blog. Nostalgia, it turns out, has a diff size.

Another perfect merge month — the second in a row, which either says
goodbye to 2025 politely or means the quality bar survived the
holidays. The row that jumps out is issues: **ninety-one opened**, the
biggest planning month of the whole run. December was for writing
things down so the new year would know what to do. The busiest PR
repos were `google-plus-posts-dumper` (29), `data-curated` (22), this
site (20), and `quilt-shop-proximity` (13).

## Notable Pull Requests

### [www-chicks-net #198: importing the Google+ archive](https://github.com/chicks-net/www-chicks-net/pull/198)

167,920 additions and 648 deletions. One hundred sixty-seven *thousand*
lines — every salvaged post from a decade on Google+, parsed, cleaned,
front-mattered, and imported into the blog in one PR. The
`google-plus` tag now has more posts than some categories I actually
maintain on purpose. This is the PR the dumper below was born to
enable, and it remains the single largest diff of my year.

### [google-plus-posts-dumper #24: the parsing engine](https://github.com/chicks-net/google-plus-posts-dumper/pull/24)

431 additions completing the post-parsing infrastructure and Markdown
generation — the engine room of the big import. The repo did
twenty-nine PRs this month, going from idea to v0.7 release to "we
have successfully archived a dead social network" in about four weeks.
Archivists gonna archive.

### [fini-infra #69: an OpenSearch cluster for web logs](https://github.com/fini-net/fini-infra/pull/69)

659 additions standing up an OpenSearch cluster for the web logs —
with a nine-comment thread, the longest of the month, working through
how the logging pipeline should actually connect. When your sites
start generating enough traffic to need log search, that's either
success or a very chatty 404 page.

### [data-curated #32: the jackpot-checker](https://github.com/chicks-net/data-curated/pull/32)

696 additions tracking lottery jackpots, with syslogd and JSON logging
options, because even my gambling side quests get production-grade
observability. The most important lesson of a data engineering career:
if you track it, it becomes a dataset, and I regret nothing.

### [fini-net/quilt-shop-proximity: a new project appears](https://github.com/fini-net/quilt-shop-proximity)

Thirteen PRs to close the month: a Godot skeleton with an iOS location
plugin, a sqlite database prepared and geocoded to 100% via
OpenStreetMap's Nominatim API, quilt shops pulled from vcq.org and the
web, California and Virginia merged into one database. The essential
question this app answers — "where is the nearest quilt shop?" — may
be the most wholesome query any of my projects has ever serviced.
