[September's recap](/posts/2025-09-30-github-recap-2025-09/) opened
the series with 144 PRs and a 97% merge rate. October decided to show
off: 108 pull requests opened, 108 merged, zero left open, zero
closed. A perfect month — the first of what would become a running
tradition. And buried in there is my favorite oddball project of the
fall: running a fifty-year-old book through my modern PR workflow,
one chapter at a time.

The busiest repos were this website (24), `fini-infra` (23), and

was infrastructure month, with the CDN and tfstate work generating the
two longest review threads of the fall. And the mythical_person_month
number is the oddball — more on that below.

## Releases Shipped

The one release of the month is a big one:
[template-repo v1.0](https://github.com/fini-net/template-repo/releases/tag/v1.0)

recaps descends from this one.

## Notable Pull Requests

### [fini-infra #41: build a DigitalOcean CDN](https://github.com/fini-net/fini-infra/pull/41)

222 additions and the review thread of the month: **twenty-five
comments**, Claude and me going back and forth over DNS, origins, and
certificate details until the CDN for the fini domains actually worked.
The companion work was just as unglamorous and just as essential — a
tfstate bucket with credentials pulled from 1Password (twelve comments)
and the web-content bucket (ten more). Infrastructure months are
measured in comment threads, and October measured up.

### [www-chicks-net #163: review of The evolution of Lua](https://github.com/chicks-net/www-chicks-net/pull/163)

851 additions and 155 deletions — the
[Lua evolution review post](/posts/2025-10-23-review-evolution-of-lua/),
an eight-comment thread workshopping a long read on how a scripting
language from Brazil quietly took over the world's game engines. Also
on the blog this month: the
[Vocal data engineer opening](/posts/2025-10-08-vocal-data-engineer-opening/)
job post, which is what happens when a hiring team lets a blogger near
the description.

### [mythical_person_month: the book project](https://github.com/chicks-net/mythical_person_month)

Twenty-two PRs, and the best niche project of my fall: carrying *The
Mythical Man-Month* — the 1975 classic, fifty years old, still in
print — through a full modern workflow. Renaming the original PDFs,
converting chapters to Markdown in careful phases (chapters 1-2, then
3-5, then 6-8, and so on), fixing false footnotes, and a just recipe
to turn the Markdown back into PDF. One chapter range per PR, reviewed
like production code, because some books deserve the full engineering
treatment. Brooks wrote about how adding people to a late project
makes it later; he never specified whether that applies to PR review,
and I have decided it does not.
